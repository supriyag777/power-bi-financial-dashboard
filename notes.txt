DOCUMENT TITLE: Strategic Plan for Enterprise Power BI Dashboard
PROJECT: Financial Analytics & Business Intelligence Implementation
AUTHOR: Business Analyst
EFFORT ESTIMATE: 32 Hours (4 Weeks)
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
1.1 Project Purpose
The primary objective of this project is to develop an enterprise-grade Power BI
dashboard that transforms raw financial operational data into interactive, real-time
insights. By automating financial reporting, this dashboard reduces manual effort
and enables data-driven decision-making across leadership teams.
1.2 Business Questions Answered
• What is our total revenue, total profit, and net profit margin percentage across global
markets?
• Which countries drive peak sales and yield the highest profitability?
• What are our revenue and profit performance trends over time?
• How do sales vary across different product lines and customer segments?
2. DATA ARCHITECTURE & FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
2.1 Data Ingestion & Source Data
• Primary Source: Financial Operations Dataset (financials)
• Data Fields: Segment, Country, Product, Discount Band, Units Sold, Sale Price,
 Gross Sales, Discounts, Sales, COGS, Profit, Date.
2.2 ETL & Data Transformations (Power Query)
• Data Type Verification: Ensure sales/financial metrics are formatted as
Decimal/Currency,
 dates as Date objects, and categorical attributes as Text.
• Data Cleansing: Remove null values, validate schema consistency, and handle missing
entries.
2.3 Data Modeling & DAX Calculation Engine
• Architecture: Star Schema approach separating dimensions from numerical facts.
• Explicit DAX Measures:
 - Total Revenue = SUM(financials[Sales])
 - Total Profit = SUM(financials[Profit])
 - Profit Margin % = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Revenue], 0)
3. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs)
• Financial Health: Total Revenue ($26.42M), Total Profit ($3.88M), Gross Profit Margin %
(14.68%).
• Market Performance: Country Sales Volume (Total Revenue by Country).
• Temporal Trends: Yearly Profit Performance (Total Profit by Year).

5. PROJECT MILESTONES & WORK BREAKDOWN STRUCTURE (32 HOURS)
Phase 1: Discovery & Data Preparation (Week 1 - 8 Hours)
• Define business questions and KPI criteria.
• Connect Power BI to source data and execute ETL transformations in Power Query.
Phase 2: Data Modeling & Measure Development (Week 2 - 8 Hours)
• Construct relational model and setup dedicated measures repository.
• Author core DAX logic for business metrics and time-intelligence analyses.
Phase 3: Visual Design & Interactivity (Week 3 - 10 Hours)
• Design canvas visual layout (KPI Cards, Bar Charts, Line Charts, Slicers).
• Configure drill-through features, cross-filtering, and UI usability standards.
Phase 4: Security, Deployment & Acceptance (Week 4 - 6 Hours)
• Implement Row-Level Security (RLS) for role-based data visibility.
• Publish report to Power BI Service workspace and complete User Acceptance Testing
(UAT).
